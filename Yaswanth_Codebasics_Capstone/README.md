# Capstone 1: AtliQ Sales & CRM Assistant

Yaswanth, Codebasics AI PM Bootcamp.

**The problem:** AtliQ doesn't have a lead problem; it has a memory problem. On 10 July 2026, 33 clients were waiting on AtliQ (median 15 days), and none of the 40 CRM records had a next follow-up date. **The solution:** Recall, an AI sales memory that reads every channel, ranks who is waiting by the founders' own rules, drafts grounded follow-ups, and proposes CRM updates for approval.

**North Star:** % of client asks and AtliQ promises closed within 2 business days (under 20% today, target 90%).
